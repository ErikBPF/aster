Usage

  • Preserve the diagonal-cut A, relative letter spacing, and raised star position.
  • Clear space: at least one full star diameter around the visible artwork. SVG viewBox padding is export padding, not the full clear-space requirement.
  • Recommended minimum full-logo width: 160 px. Use the isolated star for compact UI; check your final background at the actual size.
  • The star is one flat color, including its center. Do not add gradients, outlines, shadows or multicolor arms.
  • Use Starlight on Midnight; use Deep violet on white. Use pure-white and pure-black variants for one-color reproduction.
  • The wordmark is custom path artwork, not typeset text. Suggested product typography: system-ui sans for UI and ui-monospace for SQL.

Production notes

All logo SVGs are true vector paths and shapes without font or raster dependencies. Transparent PNGs have a genuine alpha channel. The midnight lockup and app tile intentionally include a background. Favicons use the same symbol; verify at 16 px in the target browser.

This starter kit is a clean vector reconstruction of the approved image, with normalized curves and star geometry. The exact original font was not identifiable from the image. This is a digital RGB palette; print-specific colors and trademark clearance are not included.
